ABOUT ME

From drafting
to development

My journey began with technical drawings, construction plans, and CAD. During my civil drafting diploma, I prepared construction and building service drawings, worked with bills of quantities, and gained practical experience in surveying and levelling.

Today, I’m pursuing an HND in Computer Software Engineering at ESOFT Metro Campus. I’m applying that same care and curiosity to full-stack web applications — from React interfaces to the databases and logic behind them.

I’m looking for a software development internship where I can learn from a team, contribute to real projects, and keep growing as a developer. I speak Tamil and English, and I enjoy solving problems through research, teamwork, and hands-on building.
